About me

A mindful builder at the intersection of people, design, and technology.

I am Sharon Campbell, a software engineer researching informatics at Indiana University Luddy School of Informatics, Computing, and Engineering.

My perspective

I design with a big imagination and great responsibility.

I am interested in how thoughtful technology can make complex tasks easier, information clearer, and digital spaces more welcoming. My work is grounded in human-centered design, creative problem solving, and continuous learning.

As I develop graduate research, I am building history in web development, interface design, accessibility, and the ethical use of technology. I am especially devoted to new Vitrual Reality technology and ultimate user experience design.

This website documents my new research: early experiments, completed development projects, collaborative projects, and the questions that guide my next steps into our future.
